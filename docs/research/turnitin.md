## 2. Report anatomy (as shown in Turnitin's own screenshots and guides)

Source: `img/10-guide-using-ai-writing-report-img01.orig.png` (full report), `img02` (panel zoom), `img03`–`img07` (states), `img/12-guide-access-report-img04.orig.png` (indicator tile). Terminology is quoted exactly.

### 2.1 Where the report lives
The AI report is **not a standalone tool**; it is a tab inside the Feedback Studio / Similarity Report viewer. "We have added an AI writing indicator to the Similarity Report… The indicator further links to a report that highlights the text segments that our model predicts were written or modified by an AI tool. Please note, only instructors and administrators are able to see the indicator." Students never see it ("The AI writing detection indicator and report are not visible to students. However, with the PDF download feature, instructors can download and share the AI report with students.").

### 2.2 Viewer layout (full screenshot, 823x715 source)
- **Top bar (white):** "feedback studio" wordmark left; centre = submission navigator "‹  1 of 17: Lucas Anderson ▾  ›" with the paper title beneath ("Navigating the Complex Land…"); right = "Grade [ 0 ] /100" input, then icon buttons "Download", "Settings", "Details", "Help".
- **Tab strip (second row, centred):** `Similarity 23%` · `AI Writing 56%` (active, underlined) · `Flags 0` · `Feedback` · `Grading`. Each score is a small rounded pill after the label; the AI Writing pill is cyan, Similarity's is grey.
- **Left ~63%: document canvas.** Light-grey background, white page. Sentences flagged as AI carry a **pale cyan/aqua background highlight** applied per sentence (wraps line by line; adjacent sentences merge into a block). No underline, no border, no numbering inside the text. Bottom-left floating toolbar: "Page 1 of 4" · "738 words" · zoom "– 100% +". Bottom-right: a floating Turnitin-logo button (opens "Give feedback").
- **Right ~37%: AI Writing side panel** (white card on grey), top-to-bottom:
  1. **Headline score:** "56% detected as AI" — large bold sans (≈2x body size), the number is not boxed or coloured; an ⓘ info icon follows the text.
  2. **One-line definition under the score (regular weight, grey-black):** "The percentage indicates the combined amount of likely AI-generated text as well as likely AI-generated text that was also likely revised by AI."
  3. Thin divider.
  4. **"Submission Breakdown"** (bold, with a list icon), a dark tooltip "AI-generated" is shown hovering over a bar segment.
  5. **Interactive submission breakdown bar:** a full-width, thin, rounded, outlined horizontal bar representing the whole document; page boundaries are tick marks under the bar; the left end is labelled "page 1", right end "page 4" (small italic grey). Detected regions are filled with a cyan gradient. "The bar is interactive and allows you to select each highlight to bring the corresponding text and page of the submission into focus."
  6. **Category row (the legend):** left = robot icon inside a light-cyan circle; count "2" (number of highlighted regions) then category name **"AI-generated"** (bold); sub-caption "Likely generated or likely generated and revised by AI"; right-aligned percentage "56%".
  7. **Help card:** three outlined pill buttons — full-width "ⓘ FAQs", then half-width "Resources" (book icon) and "Guides" (star icon).
  8. **Disclaimer (bottom, collapsible):** a blue link "Hide Disclaimer ▴" above small grey text: "Our AI writing assessment is designed to help educators identify text that might be prepared by a generative AI tool. It takes further scrutiny and human judgment in conjunction with an organization's specific academic policies to determine whether any academic misconduct has occurred."

### 2.3 Highlight colours and the paraphrased category
- Current guides describe a **single category**: "AI-generated detects qualifying text that was likely generated from a Large Language Model (LLM). It also detects when likely AI generated text may have been further modified by an AI bypasser, AI-paraphrasing tool or AI word spinner, such as Quillbot. This text is highlighted in a blue color in the submission breakdown bar and in the submission." The FAQ says the same colour is "cyan": "Likely AI-generated text, including text likely modified by a bypasser, is highlighted in cyan in the submission."
- The paraphrased/bypassed case is therefore **folded into the one cyan category** and surfaced only in wording ("or likely generated and revised by AI"; FAQ: "Likely AI-generated content that may have also been AI-paraphrased or bypassed will be highlighted and shown as part of the overall AI-generated content in the report."). The FAQ dates the simplification: "In July 2026, we updated our model architecture to consolidate a multi-model ensemble into a single model. This update improves and simplifies the AI writing report, maintaining a less than 1% false positive rate."
- Vestiges of the older two-category layout remain: the loading skeleton (`img04`) still shows **two** category rows, and the FAQ's indicator text still says the percentage covers "only likely AI-generated or bypassed, and likely AI-generated text that was also likely AI-paraphrased". (Prior to this consolidation the report's legend read "AI-generated only" in cyan and "AI-generated text that was likely AI-paraphrased" in purple/lavender — that older legend was **not observed** in any currently published Turnitin asset during this capture, so treat it as historical.)
- Colour notes from the screenshot: sentence highlight ≈ very pale aqua (roughly `#D9F4F8`); breakdown-bar fills ≈ cyan gradient (`#7EDBEA` → `#BFEDF4`); tab pill ≈ light cyan with dark text; the classic indicator tile uses a saturated brand blue (≈ `#0A63B5`) header with white "AI" and blue percentage text on white; the asterisk variant uses a light-blue (≈ `#DDEBF8`) rounded tile with a blue border.

### 2.4 The AI writing indicator (classic Similarity Report toolbar)
"When you open the classic Similarity Report, the AI Writing indicator appears in the toolbar on the right-side panel. To open the AI Writing Report, select the AI percentage displayed in the indicator." The tile is a small vertical rounded rectangle: solid-blue cap with white "AI", white body with the bold blue percentage (or `*%`). Colour states: "A blue indicator means the submission processed successfully and displays the percentage of qualifying text likely generated by AI." · "An asterisk (*%) appears when AI detection is below 20%…" · "A gray indicator with no percentage means the submission could not be processed…" (FAQ: "Gray with no percentage displayed (- -)") · "An error icon means the submission failed to process and the AI Writing Report cannot be opened." In the new report, the equivalent is the tab pill.

### 2.5 The asterisk rule (scores under 20%) — exact wording
- Indicator/tab: shows `*%`. Panel headline: **"*% detected as AI ⓘ"**, followed by a light-blue info box (ⓘ icon): "AI detection includes the possibility of false positives. Although some text in this submission is likely AI generated, scores below the 20% threshold are not surfaced because they have a higher likelihood of false positives." No text is highlighted in the document.
- Guide: "Our testing has found that there is a higher incidence of false positives when the percentage is between 0 and 19. In order to reduce the likelihood of misinterpretation, the AI indicator will display an asterisk (*) for percentages between 0 and 20 to call attention to the fact that the score is less reliable." and "Percentage detected as AI between 1 and 19% are not surfaced (*%) — To avoid potential incidence of false positives, no score or highlights are attributed for AI detection scores above 0% and below the 20% threshold in the report. When this occurs, it is now indicated with an asterisk (*%) and no percentage is attributed." Legacy note: "If your report was generated before July 8, 2024, then you may still see a numerical score below 20%."
- Help-center article "Why is the AI Writing Detection report score showing as *%?": "To avoid potential incidences of false positives, Turnitin does not display specific numerical scores or source highlights for AI detection levels within the **1% to 19% range**. … **The indicator:** An asterisk symbol (*%) is displayed. **The report:** No specific text blocks will be highlighted in the document view, and no numerical score will be attributed to the report."
- 0% is a distinct state: "0% detected as AI — After processing, Turnitin's AI detection did not identify any qualifying text in the submission as likely generated by an AI writing and/or AI text altering tool."
- 20–100%: "The submission has been processed successfully. The percentage displayed at the top of the report indicates the amount of qualifying text within the submission that Turnitin's AI writing detection has identified as likely AI-generated or likely AI-generated text, and then was modified by an AI paraphrase tool."

### 2.6 "Qualifying text" and the percentage definition — exact wording
- "The overall percentage detected as AI indicates the qualifying text (prose sentences contained in a long-form writing format) within the submitted document that Turnitin's AI writing detection model determines could be generated by AI or could be generated by AI and could be further modified using an AI paraphraser tool or an AI bypasser tool."
- "Qualifying text (prose sentences contained in long-form writing format) means individual sentences contained in paragraphs that make up a longer piece of written work, such as an essay, a dissertation, or an article, etc. The model does not reliably detect AI-generated text in the form of non-prose, such as poetry, scripts, or code, nor does it detect short-form/unconventional writing such as bullet points, tables, or annotated bibliographies."
- "This percentage is not necessarily the percentage of the entire submission. If text within the submission is not considered long-form prose text, it will not be included." / "This means that a document containing several different writing types would result in a disparity between the percentage and the highlights."
- Independence from similarity: "The percentage generated by Turnitin's AI writing detection model is different from and independent of the similarity score. AI writing highlights are not visible in the Similarity Report."

### 2.7 Other panel states (exact strings)
- **Loading:** grey skeleton bars mirroring the panel (heading + line, divider, breakdown row, bar, two category rows with right-aligned value chips). Guide: "The loading state indicates that the AI writing detection request is currently processing. Generating the report results can take several minutes."
- **Processing error:** red circular "!" icon; "There was a problem processing AI writing detection for this submission." / "Resubmit this document to try processing again. If this issue persists, contact your administrator."
- **Unavailable / file requirements:** heading "AI writing detection is unavailable for this submission"; blue info box "Reasons could include:" • "Submission file is an unsupported file type" • "Submission text is in an unsupported language" • "Qualifying text is either fewer than 300 words or more than 30,000 words".
- **Disabled at submission time:** grey ⓘ icon; "AI writing detection was disabled at the time of submission" / "Resubmit to generate an AI writing report."
- **File requirements (guide list):** "File size must be less than 100 MB · File must have at least 300 words of prose text in a long-form writing format · File must not exceed 30,000 words · File must be written in a supported language: English, Spanish, Japanese, Arabic · Accepted file types: .docx, .pdf, .txt, .rtf".

### 2.8 Accuracy / false-positive disclaimers (exact wording used across pages)
- Guide intro: "Our AI writing detection model may not always be accurate (it may misidentify human-written, AI-generated, and AI-paraphrased text), so it should not be used as the sole basis for adverse actions against a student. It takes further scrutiny and human judgment in conjunction with an organization's application of its specific academic policies to determine whether academic misconduct has occurred."
- FAQ: "We strive to maximize the effectiveness of our detector while keeping our false positive rate - incorrectly identifying fully human-written text as AI-generated - under 1% for documents with over 20% of AI writing. In other words, we might flag a human-written document as AI-written for one out of every 100 fully-human written documents."
- FAQ: "In order to maintain this low rate of 1% for false positives, there is a chance that we might miss some AI written text in a document. We're comfortable with that since we do not want to incorrectly highlight human-written text as AI-written. For example, if we identify that 50% of a document is likely written by an AI tool, it could contain as much as 65% AI writing."
- FAQ: "Sometimes false positives … can include content without a lot of structural variation, text that literally repeats itself, or text that has been paraphrased without developing new ideas." and "In shorter documents where there are only a few hundred words, the prediction will be mostly "all or nothing" because we're predicting on a single segment without the opportunity to overlap."
- How-to-review guide: "It is not meant to provide definitive answers in isolation. More important than any tool is the educator who sees the score and makes decisions balancing this information with their personal knowledge of their students, their work, and institutional policy." / "When educators look at the AI writing score and utilize it as a single data point rather than a definitive response, then it is being used as intended."
- Method (FAQ): "sentences from the submission are extracted and segmented into overlapping sections for prediction analysis. Each segment is classified by the AI detection model and given a value between 0 and 1… Each qualifying sentence within these segments inherits the segment's score… These sentence scores are further aggregated and used to compute the overall document AI writing score." Also: "Our model is not explicitly programmed to evaluate specific signals such as "burstiness," "perplexity," or other individual metrics…".
- Feedback affordance: "You can provide feedback via the 'feedback' button found in the AI writing report… click or tap the Turnitin logo in the bottom right corner of the viewer and select Give feedback."
- Download: "The AI detection report can be downloaded as a PDF via the 'download' button located in the right-hand corner of the report."

## 4. Copy patterns worth borrowing (honest, institution-like tone)

1. **Headline the score as a measurement of text, then define it in one sentence right underneath.** "56% detected as AI" + "The percentage indicates the combined amount of likely AI-generated text as well as likely AI-generated text that was also likely revised by AI."
2. **Hedge with "likely" in every label, including the legend.** Category name "AI-generated", caption "Likely generated or likely generated and revised by AI"; marketing copy consistently says "likely AI-generated" rather than "AI-generated".
3. **Suppress unreliable low scores with an asterisk and explain it in place, not in a footnote.** "*% detected as AI" + "AI detection includes the possibility of false positives. Although some text in this submission is likely AI generated, scores below the 20% threshold are not surfaced because they have a higher likelihood of false positives."
4. **Define the denominator ("qualifying text") and admit the percentage may not match the highlights.** "Qualifying text (prose sentences contained in long-form writing format) means individual sentences contained in paragraphs…" and "This percentage is not necessarily the percentage of the entire submission."
5. **Keep a standing, collapsible disclaimer at the bottom of the panel ("Hide Disclaimer").** "…may not always be accurate (i.e., our AI models may produce either false positive results or false negative results), so it should not be used as the sole basis for adverse actions against a student."
6. **Quantify the error rate and state the condition it holds under, then translate it.** "…under 1% for documents with over 20% of AI writing. In other words, we might flag a human-written document as AI-written for one out of every 100 fully-human written documents."
7. **Own the trade-off you chose.** "In order to maintain this low rate of 1% for false positives, there is a chance that we might miss some AI written text in a document. We're comfortable with that… if we identify that 50% of a document is likely written by an AI tool, it could contain as much as 65% AI writing."
8. **Say explicitly what the tool does not decide.** "Turnitin does not make a determination of misconduct, rather it provides data for the educators to make an informed decision based on their academic and institutional policies." / "a single data point rather than a definitive response".
9. **Write empty/error states as reasons plus a next step.** "AI writing detection is unavailable for this submission" → "Reasons could include:" (unsupported file type / unsupported language / "Qualifying text is either fewer than 300 words or more than 30,000 words"); "Resubmit this document to try processing again. If this issue persists, contact your administrator."
10. **Name the known failure modes for the reader.** "false positives… can include content without a lot of structural variation, text that literally repeats itself, or text that has been paraphrased without developing new ideas" and "In shorter documents where there are only a few hundred words, the prediction will be mostly "all or nothing"".
11. **Name the adversary plainly and fold it into one category rather than a scary second colour.** "AI bypassers (also called humanizers)"; "Likely AI-generated content that may have also been AI-paraphrased or bypassed will be highlighted and shown as part of the overall AI-generated content in the report."
12. **Use consultative, low-pressure CTAs and an inclusion/exclusion bullet in every price card.** "Chat with an expert", "Speak to an expert", "Schedule a call", "Ready to talk? Chat with an expert."; iThenticate cards list "Credits valid for 12 months · Includes 5 free revisions · AI writing detection not available" so the buyer sees what is *not* included before paying.
